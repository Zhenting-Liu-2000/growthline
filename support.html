隐私政策 · Privacy policy

需要登录或联网同步吗？ / Do I need sign-in or sync?

不需要应用账号。可在系统登录 iCloud 后，自主开启应用内同步；请先核对当前系统账号。关闭同步不等于删除云端副本。

No app account is required. You can opt into in-app sync using the system iCloud account. Turning sync off does not erase uploaded copies.

如何备份？ / How do I back up?

进入“设置 › 数据 › 备份与恢复 › 导出备份”。收到保存成功提示后，再点“另存一份到…”存到独立位置。仅留在本应用文件夹里的备份会随卸载删除。

Open Settings > Data > Backup & restore > Export backup. After the save succeeds, use Also save to… to keep a copy outside the app folder. A copy only inside that folder is removed when you delete the app.

恢复是合并吗？ / Does restore merge records?

不是。先核对预览中的条数与日期，再确认替换。当前受支持资料会变成备份中的内容；备份中没有的内容会被移除。先给当前资料留一份独立备份。

No. Check the preview counts and dates before confirming replacement. Supported current records are replaced by the backup; content absent from it is removed. Keep a separate backup of your current data first.

恢复后为什么不同步？ / Why is sync paused after restore?

恢复后先暂停同步，给你核对资料的机会。到“设置 › 数据 › iCloud 同步”决定是否上传；上传可能替换其他设备上的资料。

Sync pauses so you can check the restored data. In Settings > Data > iCloud sync, choose whether to upload; doing so can replace records on other devices.

恢复失败后怎么办？ / What if restore fails?

保留应用与现有文件，记录错误提示。若提示回滚未完成，不要反复导入或卸载；先保留可用备份并联系支持。

Keep the app and existing files and record the error. If rollback is incomplete, do not repeatedly import or uninstall. Preserve available backups and contact support.

为什么预测暂停或与实测不同？ / Why is a prediction paused or different from a measurement?

预测是估计值，依赖测量口径、培养条件与参数。信息不足、实测下降、检测限或计算不稳定等情况可能暂停预测。请按当前提示补实测；不要把历史估计当作当前结果。

Predictions are estimates that depend on measurement basis, culture conditions and parameters. Missing information, observed decline, detection limits or numerical instability can pause them. Follow the current message and record a measurement; do not substitute an old estimate.

计算结果能直接作为操作方案吗？ / Can I treat a calculation as a protocol?

不能。先独立核对输入、单位、盐型、水合物、最终体积及本实验条件，并以实验室批准的 SOP 为准。

No. Independently check inputs, units, salt or hydrate form, final volume and your experimental conditions against your lab’s approved SOP.

提醒没出现怎么办？ / Why did a reminder not appear?

在系统设置检查通知权限与预览设置，并在应用中检查提醒选项。提醒是辅助，请自行查看实验计划和细胞实测，不把提醒当作唯一依据。

Check system notification permissions and app reminder options. Reminders are supplementary; check your plan and measurements directly.

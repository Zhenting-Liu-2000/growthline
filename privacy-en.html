Privacy Policy — Benchmate

Scope and contact

Benchmate (Benchmate) is a free first-release experiment management app for biomedical students. This policy covers experiment plans, cell cultures, animal cage records, research calculations, photos, backups and reminders. Operator: Zhenting Liu. Support and privacy contact: Zhenting.Liu.2000@gmail.com. Effective date: October 28, 2026.

Local records

Experiment names, steps, dates, notes, cell measurements, culture conditions, cage records, calculation inputs and photos are initially stored on your device. Cell growth values and curves are model estimates, not measurements or clinical advice. Widgets read the summaries they need from the app’s shared container. Only enter personal or confidential research information you are authorized to handle.

No app sign-in; optional iCloud sync

You do not need an app account or Sign in with Apple. If you explicitly enable in-app iCloud sync, supported experiment and research records and photos sync through Apple’s iCloud services to the private database of the iCloud account signed in on your device. Account status, connectivity, permissions and storage can affect sync. Turning sync off stops this record-sync channel but does not immediately delete uploaded copies or disable system backup or calendar sync you enabled separately. After restoring a backup, record sync is paused. Check the restored data before deciding whether to upload it; uploading can replace data on your other devices with the restored state.

Camera, photos, calendar and notifications

The app uses the camera or calendar when you initiate the relevant action and grant permission. The system photo picker provides only the images you select, not access to your entire library. Images may retain identifying content or metadata; check them before use. With calendar access and the relevant feature enabled, the app can read events and create, update or remove linked events. Your selected calendar account may sync independently. Local reminders can show experiment titles on the lock screen; manage notifications and previews in system settings.

Backups, restore and separate copies

Use Settings > Data > Backup & restore to export supported records as a .glbackup file. It is first saved in Files > On My iPhone > Benchmate. Deleting the app removes this app folder. Before reinstalling or changing phones, use Also save to… to keep a separate copy in a trusted location. Backups may contain records and photos; protect them appropriately. The app does not provide password encryption for the backup package. Restore requires confirmation and replaces supported current data with the backup, removing content absent from it; it is not a merge. The app attempts to save a safety copy first. If an error occurs, preserve existing files and contact support.

Support email and external services

If you choose to email us, the recipient and relevant email services process your sender address, message and attachments to handle your request and necessary communication. Send only redacted issue descriptions, not a full database, passwords or unauthorized research information. We keep support information for as long as needed to handle the request. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion; we will explain any continued retention required by law or a security dispute. If you open a source website or save a file to a cloud service, that service processes the request under its own terms.

Advertising and analytics

This first release does not use advertising tracking or provide research records to developer-operated analytics or automatic crash-reporting services. Apple system backup and diagnostics, and your selected calendar, file or email service, may process data under their respective settings. These system services are distinct from the app’s own record sync.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local records remain until you delete the relevant content or remove the app’s data. Editing or deleting a record does not automatically erase exported backups, restore safety copies, system backups or other separate copies. Uninstalling the app does not delete every copy. You can export supported records, manage system permissions and ask us to handle support information we actually hold. We cannot directly retrieve research records stored only on your device or in your private iCloud database.

Audience and updates

The app supports research study and work. It is not directed at children and does not provide human diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions. Follow your institution’s data, laboratory safety and ethics requirements. We will update the policy and relevant notices when data practices change. The current policy and contact details are available on this site.
